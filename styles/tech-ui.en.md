# Dark tech UI (id: tech-ui)

中文:[tech-ui.md](tech-ui.md)

## Rendering language
A deep blue-black background, translucent panels and thin-line frames, a blue glow on key elements; geometric, clean, little decoration; text is part of the interface.

## Materials and post
Slightly transparent panels with thin strokes, glow (blur + lighter blending), optionally very light scanlines or noise; no paper texture.

## Font tendencies
A geometric sans (Space Grotesk) for titles and English, monospace (JetBrains Mono) for numbers and terminals, Noto Sans SC for all Chinese. No handwriting layer.

## Component looks
Terminal windows, data panels, counters, status capsules, quote cards, source credits; dialog boxes are rounded cards.

## Motion feel
Typing letter by letter, counting numbers, cards sliding in / flipping, light sweeps; fast and crisp.

## How characters are drawn
- A code-drawn mascot as the operator (a rim glow helps it sit on the dark background).
- Generated characters (untried): prompt for flat, cool ambient light, a blue rim light at the edges; add glow in code.

## Scenes it can draw
Used in: terminal-tech (A1, A2). Suggested: a modern data dashboard, launch-event style explainers.

## Pitfalls
Never imitate a real product's UI; small text on dark backgrounds must be large enough (for phones).
