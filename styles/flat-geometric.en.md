# Flat geometric (id: flat-geometric)

中文:[flat-geometric.md](flat-geometric.md)

## Rendering language
Clean science diagrams: a dark solid background, no outlines, two tones per shape (light side / dark side), geometric forms, one accent color.

## Materials and post
Hardly any materials; very light grain optional.

## Font tendencies
**Deliberately only two**: one crisp display font (Smiley Sans) for titles, labels, numbers and bubbles; Noto Sans SC for small source credits.
No handwriting, typing or printed-original layers — use a quote card if needed.

## Component looks
Capsule labels (dark blue on yellow), chapter cards, causal-chain arrows, outline-free rounded bubbles, yellow highlight blocks instead of red-pen circling.

## Motion feel
Fade + rise; move the camera along the causal chain; restrained.

## How characters are drawn
- In code: geometric characters, simplified organs (a heart with a face, etc.).
- Generated characters: the same constraints as flat-illustration (no outlines, shading one step darker in the same hue).

## Scenes it can draw
Used in: anatomy diagram (anatomy-diagram). Suggested: process diagrams, comparison charts, any mechanism explainer.

## Pitfalls
On one mechanism explainer the author went through a rejected "short-drama look" and a rejected "hand-drawn high-detail texture look" before landing here — show stills to choose from before making the full video.
