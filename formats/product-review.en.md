# New product / feature explained (id: product-review)

中文:[product-review.md](product-review.md)

## In one line
A new product, model or feature comes out: what's new, what the official examples show, how it compares with the previous version, how ordinary people should use it.

## Topic signals
- The launch is still hot; official examples or public evaluations to cite.
- **Note**: pure news got a lukewarm audience response (the decision-maker explicitly doesn't want pure news); if you do it, include "how to use it" or an original comparison, not just a summary.

## Narrative skeleton (one finished video, 7 paragraphs, about 6′)
| Paragraph | Role |
|---|---|
| 1 | One-line verdict + the most visible change |
| 2–4 | New features one by one, each with an official example / public number (with source) |
| 5 | Comparison with the previous version / competitors |
| 6 | New ways to use it (schematic animation) |
| 7 | Who should use it, and how |

## Required shots / through-line elements
- Every number carries a source credit; official examples quoted as they are.
- Demos use schematic animation in our own style, not imitations of real product UIs, labeled "schematic".

## Fact and platform risks
- Look carefully at what an official comparison chart actually compares (measured: three official comparisons compared speed, not detail — the script was corrected).
- No URLs, no "official site" / "launch page" (measured: throttled for it).

## Suitable scenes
Validated: terminal-tech (one finished video).

## Cases
- One new-model explainer: 343.7 s of narration; a comparison with the previous version added after the cut; throttled for mentioning a website, fixed by re-rendering the visuals only.
