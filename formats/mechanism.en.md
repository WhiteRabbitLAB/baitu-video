# Mechanism explainer (id: mechanism)

中文:[mechanism.md](mechanism.md)

## In one line
Starting from a meme or a common feeling, explain how it really works (body, technology or society), ending with safety / usage red lines. The author's nutrition channel used it for a body mechanism.

## Topic signals
- Ready-made traffic (measured: opening with a line from a trending short drama).
- The mechanism can be drawn as one causal chain (a valve loosens → things flow back → that section has no protection).
- The meme is only a hook; the content has nothing to do with it, and we don't judge what's in the meme.

## Narrative skeleton (one finished video, 11 paragraphs, 4:35)
| Paragraph | Role |
|---|---|
| 1 | The meme's line + one bridging sentence to the real topic |
| 2 | Correct the name (the folk name is misleading: it actually hurts somewhere else) |
| 3–6 | The mechanism chain, one link per paragraph (each with a classic study) |
| 7–8 | Aggravating factors / everyday situations |
| 9 | Authoritative advice (guidelines) |
| 10 | A short history of drugs / treatments |
| 11 | Warning signs; rule out dangerous conditions first (serious, no jokes) |

## Required shots / through-line elements
- One schematic anatomy diagram throughout; each paragraph lights up one part of it.
- The safety paragraph stays serious.

## Transition preferences
Move the camera along the causal chain (from one organ to the next).

## Fact and platform risks
- Medical advice follows the guideline's wording; warning signs are mandatory.
- Don't use the meme's original audio (throttling / copyright); imitate it with your own TTS voice.

## Suitable scenes
Validated: anatomy-diagram scene + flat-geometric style (third version). Rejected: short-drama look, hand-drawn high-detail texture look (decision-maker: "animation quality too poor", "ugly assets, ugly fonts").

## Cases
- One video, three versions. Lesson: **agree on the style with the decision-maker before drawing**, don't build the whole video first; the decision-maker finally said "an explainer just needs to explain clearly — it doesn't need to be a short drama".
