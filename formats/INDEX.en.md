中文:[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)

Format = narrative skeleton + paragraph rhythm + required shots. Independent of scene and style: one format can go with different scenes (see each scene's "suitable formats").
Cases come from the author's two channels (nutrition science, practical AI); the formats themselves aren't topic-bound.

## Counter-example (don't do this)
- **Argumentative essay** (a video about "AI makes work look better, so why does it all look the same?"): all argument, no story line, and a closing demo that "looked different and was on-topic, but had lost its taste" — the whole video was judged a failure. Lesson: put the opinion inside a story or a real test; **the demonstration itself must be good**, or it undercuts the point.

## Adding a format
1. Copy [_template.md](_template.en.md) to `formats/<id>.md` and fill in every section.
2. Validate the rhythm with at least one sample (paragraphs 1–2); register it in the table with status "sample-validated". After a full video, change it to "full-video-validated".
3. A format does **not** specify the visual world, colors or fonts — those belong to scenes and styles.
