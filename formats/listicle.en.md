# List (id: listicle) 🧪

中文:[listicle.md](listicle.md)

## In one line
N (3–7) parallel tips, each with a small hook and a conclusion you can use right away; fast-paced, suits vertical, and viewers take away a list worth screenshotting.

## Topic signals
- The content is naturally parallel ("5 ways to ask so AI makes up less", "4 supermarket snacks that look healthy but aren't").
- Each item stands on its own and **has its own visual moment** (a comparison, a number, an action).
- Counter-examples: items form a causal chain (that's mechanism); only 1–2 real items and the rest filler (better as a short).

## Narrative skeleton (vertical 60–90 s / landscape 2–3′)
| Paragraph | Role | Typical content | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook: conclusion first + the count | "These 5 — most people get number 3 wrong" | 5–8% |
| 2…N+1 | One item each: the number slams in → a concrete example / comparison → one-line conclusion | Big number + example + conclusion card | 12–18% each |
| Second to last | The whole list gathered into one (screenshot-able) | N lines lighting up one by one | 8% |
| Last | A question for the comments | "Know a sixth one?" | 3% |

**Order**: the strongest item first (to hold viewers), the second strongest last (to close); the middle by what viewers care about most.

## Required shots / through-line elements
- **A permanent number / progress indicator** (3 / 5): at the top in vertical; can merge into the chapter progress bar in landscape.
- Numbers slam in with kinetic type (shared component `entrance: 'slam'`, small words `'slide'`), then pin in place.
- Every item's conclusion card uses the same layout, assembled into the final list at the end (the through-line element).

## Transition preferences
Between items use **fill-zoom** or **bands** (fast section changes; both in the shared components); within an item, cut, on the breath between two sentences.
The final-list paragraph uses **morph**: each conclusion card shrinks and slots into the list.

## Fact and platform risks
- Every item goes through the fact sheet separately (lists are where an unchecked item most easily slips in).
- Hooks like "most people get number X wrong" need evidence; otherwise rephrase without the comparison.
- Douyin vertical: key text ≥ 28 px (readable on a phone), each item ≤ 15 s; no URLs (channel rule).

## Suitable scenes
Worth trying: info-cards, kinetic type (a roadmap style, essential for vertical), cartoon-ui.
