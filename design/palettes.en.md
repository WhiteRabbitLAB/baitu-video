## Color principles
- One accent color means one thing (the protagonist's faction / the key number); don't spray it everywhere.
- Flat styles: the dark side = one step darker in the same hue, never darkened with black.
- A character entering a scene gets graded to the scene's light once (see ../characters/act-pipeline.md §7), or it looks like a sticker.
