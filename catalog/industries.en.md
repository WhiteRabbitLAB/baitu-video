# Question two: what field

中文:[industries.md](industries.md)

The field mainly affects three things: **the metaphors and props in the picture (scene), where facts are checked, and the red lines**. A field is a tag, not a folder — one scene can serve several fields.
The split follows Bilibili's knowledge-section sub-categories (as set up in 2020: popular science, social science & humanities, finance, campus study, careers, DIY tech), broken down further for our needs.

Add a row: common purposes, popular references, suggested scenes ("to build: …" if none), fact sources, red lines, status.
