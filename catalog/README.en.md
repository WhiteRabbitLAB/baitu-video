## Status marks (used throughout)
| Mark | Meaning | How it's decided |
|---|---|---|
| ✅ Doable | A finished video exists, or a nearly identical combination has run | There's a case (say which episode) |
| 🧪 Needs testing | Doable with code + image generation + public-domain material, but no sample yet | Make a sample, the decision-maker reviews it ⇒ ✅ |
| ⛔ Not doable for now | Depends on capabilities we don't have | On-camera people, filmed experiments, lots of licensed footage, heavy 3D, real interviews |

⛔ and judgments like "code alone can't render it in detail" are **capability limits**, not permanent: when the capability arrives (a stronger model, a new generation tool), re-test, and on success change it to 🧪 or ✅.
Update the status after every episode or every new combination you try.

## The hard line: can we make it?
Can the picture be made **entirely** from "code drawing + image generation (characters, illustrations) + public-domain / self-generated material"?
- Yes ⇒ ✅ or 🧪.
- The core visuals must be real people, live footage or licensed film footage ⇒ ⛔; the usual conversion is "keep its narrative structure, replace the visuals with what we can make" (e.g. a filmed experiment → an animated retelling).
