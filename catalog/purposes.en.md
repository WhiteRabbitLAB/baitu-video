# Question one: what do you want to make (purpose)

中文:[purposes.md](purposes.md)

Purpose decides the narrative skeleton (format). In the same field, an explainer and a tutorial have completely different skeletons; the same format works across fields.

| Purpose | What viewers take away | Popular references (form) | Our formats | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Science explainer** | Finally understanding a phenomenon / mechanism | Huixingzhen (pure animated infographics), Kurzgesagt (flat animation), Bidao (presenter + graphics) | [mechanism](../formats/mechanism.en.md), [experiment](../formats/experiment.en.md), [case-file](../formats/case-file.en.md), [spread-history](../formats/spread-history.en.md), [evolution](../formats/evolution.en.md) | ✅ nutrition N3–N9 |
| **True story** | A real story about people and events | Xiaoyuehan Kehan (archival footage montage), TED-Ed (animated stories, a new style each episode) | [experiment](../formats/experiment.en.md), [timeline-epic](../formats/timeline-epic.en.md), [case-file](../formats/case-file.en.md) | ✅ N8, an art-history feature |
| **Tutorial · using a tool** | Learning to do one specific thing | Software / AI tool tutorials (screen recording + annotations) | [howto-guide](../formats/howto-guide.en.md), [story-method-test](../formats/story-method-test.en.md) | ✅ AI A2, A6 |
| **Tutorial · learning a concept** | Truly understanding an abstract idea | 3Blue1Brown (objects that morph continuously), Khan Academy (handwritten board) | [concept-lesson](../formats/concept-lesson.en.md) (concept → intuition → derivation → example) | 🧪 skeleton written, no sample yet; a natural fit for code |
| **Review & comparison** | Which one to pick | Gadget reviews, AI model shoot-outs | [product-review](../formats/product-review.en.md); [versus](../formats/versus.en.md) (A vs B point by point) | ✅ A1; versus 🧪 skeleton written, no sample yet |
| **Opinion** | Seeing something from a new angle | Banfo Xianren (memes + footage + sharp copy), Vox (collage-style argument) | Put the opinion inside a story or a real test: [story-method-test](../formats/story-method-test.en.md) | 🧪; a pure argumentative essay is the **counter-example** (A4 failed) |
| **List** | A screenshot-able list | "N things…", rankings | [listicle](../formats/listicle.en.md) (hook → one visual per item → summary table) | 🧪 skeleton written, no sample yet; suits vertical |
| **News roundup** | What just happened | News explainers, weekly digests | A variant of [product-review](../formats/product-review.en.md) | 🧪 low priority: the decision-maker explicitly doesn't want pure news |

## How to use
- Pick a purpose → see the suggested formats → go to the field table (industries.md) for scenes → pick a style.
- What you want isn't in the table: start from the closest row and add a row here afterwards (reference works, format, status).

## Formats with a skeleton, waiting for their first sample

2. **versus**: reviews need it; fixed structure, easy to template.
